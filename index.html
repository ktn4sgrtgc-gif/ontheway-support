SUPPORT

Common questions

Where are my trails and photos stored?

They are stored inside the app on your iPhone. The developer does not operate an account system or upload your trail, photo or video library to a private server.

How do I create a trail film?

Open Tracks, select a recorded or imported route, then choose Create a Trail Film. You can preview the result before choosing a subscription or exporting it.

What are the trail-film limits?

A trail film can be up to 3 minutes long. Each source video contributes at most its first 30 seconds. A track can contain up to 50 photos and videos, but the finished 3-minute film only uses the moments that fit. Your original source media is retained even when a film uses a shorter excerpt.

How do I add photos after a walk?

Open the saved track and choose Import Photos / Videos. Photos and videos can have their own note and are placed on the route using location and capture time.

Why is my location not being recorded?

Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → On the Way and allow location while using the app. Background location is only requested when you start recording.

How do I restore Pro?

Open the Pro tab, sign in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, then tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or visit Apple’s subscription page. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Where is the exported film?

Use Save to Photos to add the film to your photo library, or use the iOS share sheet to save it to Files or another app.

Can I move my data to another phone?

The current release stores data locally. Export important GPX routes and finished films before removing the app or moving to a new device. Cross-device sync is not included in the initial release.
