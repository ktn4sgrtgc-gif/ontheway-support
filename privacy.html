Support Privacy

PRIVACY FIRST

Your journey stays yours.

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Overview

On the Way is a local-first trail journal. You do not need an account. The developer does not operate a server that receives your trail, photos, videos, notes, precise location history or exported films.

Data stored on your device

The app stores trail coordinates, timestamps, distance information, place names, temperature entries, photo and video notes, selected avatar images and generated films in its private app container on your iPhone. Photos and videos you select remain in your photo library unless you explicitly ask the app to save a generated film there.

Deleting a trail or moment removes its app-associated media. Uninstalling the app removes its local container. Exported files and media already saved to Photos remain under your control.

Location and maps

Location is used to record a route and to place photos on a map. Tracking begins only after you tap Start Recording. Location access can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

The app uses Apple MapKit and Apple geocoding services to display maps and resolve place names. Those system services may send map queries and coordinates to Apple as described in Apple’s privacy policy. The developer does not receive a copy of that map activity.

Photos, videos and generated films

Media is processed on the device. The app reads capture date and optional GPS metadata to arrange media on a route. Trail films are rendered locally and are saved to Photos only after you request it. To limit device resources, the app uses at most the first 30 seconds of each source video and limits a generated film to 3 minutes; the original source file is not altered.

Purchases

On the Way Pro subscriptions, renewals, refunds and purchase restoration are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app receives subscription status from Apple but does not receive or store payment card details.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The initial release contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, cross-app tracking or data broker integration. The developer does not sell personal information.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell or rent trail, photo, video or location data. Data can leave the device only through actions you initiate, such as saving a film to Photos, sharing a file, exporting GPX or using an Apple system service such as Maps or App Store purchases.

Children

On the Way is a general-purpose travel journal and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not create child accounts or knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Change or revoke Location, Photos, Camera and Microphone permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Delete trails or moments in the app.
  • Remove saved files from Photos or Files using the system apps.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription from your Apple Account.

Support website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs under its own privacy policy. The site does not add analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Changes and contact

If a future version introduces accounts, cloud sync or new third-party services, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before those services are released.

For privacy questions, open a support issue at github.com/ktn4sgrtgc-gif/ontheway-support . Do not include private photos, precise home addresses or purchase receipts.
